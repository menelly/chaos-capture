# 🔥📸 Chaos Capture (2025 photo app: a starting point, never finished)

**What this is:** the first few hours of a planned desktop and phone app that would move your photos straight from your phone to your own computer over home Wi-Fi, with no cloud service in between.

## 💡 The idea

- 📱 take photos on your phone
- 📶 the phone finds your computer on the local network by itself
- 💻 photos go directly to the desktop app, which organises and searches them
- ☁️ no Google Photos, no uploads, nobody else holding your pictures

The full plan is in `# 🔥 CHAOS CAPTURE - Project Management .md` and `TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## 📍 State: honest version

**This is a skeleton, not an app.** It's the planning documents plus a freshly created Tauri project (`chaos-capture-app/`) with a themed front page and the template's sample "greet" button. None of the photo features were built. Both commits are from 2025-08-13, and work stopped there.

⚠️ Don't confuse it with the later **Chaos Capture** in [`chaos-capabilities`](https://github.com/menelly/chaos-capabilities), which is a different, working tool (dictation with big friendly buttons).
